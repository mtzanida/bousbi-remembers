The Moon Garden

Nia the little fox and Pip the sleepy owl found a silver watering can beside a moonflower.
The moonflower could only open when someone shared a kind memory. Nia remembered
helping Pip repair his tiny blue umbrella. One petal opened, revealing a golden seed.
They promised to plant the seed beside the pond the next evening. Pip kept it safe
inside a green pouch. They went home before planting it, wondering what would grow.
